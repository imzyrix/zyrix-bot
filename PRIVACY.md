# Privacy Policy for Zyrix Bot

**Effective Date:** May 28, 2026  
**Last Updated:** May 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy governs the collection, use, processing, storage, and disclosure of information by Zyrix Bot (referred to as "Zyrix", "the Bot", "we", "us", or "our"). Zyrix is a Discord application developed and operated by imzyrix.

By inviting Zyrix Bot to your Discord server, using its commands, or interacting with its features, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and consented to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

---

## 1. Introduction and Scope

Zyrix Bot is a multi-purpose Discord bot offering features such as advanced moderation, AI-powered chat, music playback, games, utility tools, and server management. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle data in compliance with applicable data protection laws, including but not limited to the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and other relevant jurisdictional requirements.

This policy applies to all users who interact with Zyrix Bot, including server administrators, moderators, and regular members.

---

## 2. Definitions

- **Personal Data**: Any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person.
- **Discord Data**: User IDs, Server IDs, Channel IDs, usernames, avatars, and other information provided by the Discord API.
- **Processing**: Any operation performed on personal data, whether automated or not.
- **Controller**: imzyrix, the developer responsible for determining the purposes and means of processing data.

---

## 3. Information We Collect

### 3.1 Automatically Collected Data
- Discord User ID, Server ID, Channel ID, Role IDs
- Username, Display Name, and Avatar URL (cached temporarily)
- Command usage data and interaction timestamps
- Error logs and debugging information

### 3.2 Moderation and Security Features
- Messages processed by AutoMod, Anti-Nuke, Anti-Spam, and Anti-Raid systems
- Moderation actions (warnings, mutes, kicks, bans, timeouts)
- Audit log events and server configuration changes
- Violation records and user history for repeat offenders

### 3.3 AI Chat Features
- User messages sent to the AI chatbot
- Conversation context and history (temporarily stored for coherent responses)
- All AI interactions are processed via third-party API (Groq)

### 3.4 Music and Entertainment Features
- Music queue data, currently playing track information
- Voice channel activity
- Game session data (Blackjack, Chess, Trivia, etc.)

### 3.5 Configuration and Settings
- Prefix settings, feature toggles, permission configurations
- Custom welcome/leave messages, auto-role settings, logging channels

We do **not** collect:
- Passwords
- Payment information
- Direct Messages (DMs)
- Email addresses
- Phone numbers

## 4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

We process your data based on the following legal grounds:
- **Contractual Necessity**: Processing is required to provide the bot services you requested by inviting it.
- **Legitimate Interests**: Improving bot functionality, ensuring server security, debugging issues, and preventing abuse.
- **Legal Obligation**: Complying with applicable laws and responding to lawful requests from authorities.
- **Consent**: Where we process data beyond the above bases (e.g., optional AI features), we rely on your explicit consent, which can be withdrawn at any time.

We use collected information to:

## 8. Data Storage, Retention, and Security

- Data is stored on secure servers with industry-standard encryption.
- We implement technical and organizational measures to protect data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.
- **Retention Periods**:
  - Temporary caches (AI context, music queues): Deleted after session ends or within 24-48 hours.
  - Moderation logs: Retained until manually cleared or upon bot removal.
  - Configuration data: Retained as long as the bot remains in the server.
  - Anonymized analytics: Retained indefinitely for service improvement.

Upon removal of the bot from a server, we make reasonable efforts to delete associated server-specific data.

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

- **Right to Rectification** – Correct inaccurate data
- **Right to Erasure** ("Right to be Forgotten") – Request deletion of your data
