# AI & Games

Zyrix Bot integrates advanced AI capabilities and a wide array of interactive games to enhance user engagement and provide intelligent assistance within your Discord server.

## 🤖 AI Chatbot

Our AI chatbot is powered by the cutting-edge Groq API, utilizing the `mixtral-8x7b-32768` model to deliver intelligent and responsive conversations. The AI is designed to be highly interactive and helpful.

### AI Capabilities:

*   **Chatbot Triggers**: Engage with the AI using specific triggers like `chatbot`, `$`, or `soniye`.
*   **Internet Access**: The AI has internet access, allowing it to provide up-to-date information and contextually relevant responses.
*   **NSFW Filter**: A built-in filter ensures that conversations remain appropriate for all audiences.
*   **Direct Message Support**: Interact with the AI directly in your private messages.
*   **Smart Mention Response**: The bot intelligently responds when mentioned in conversations.
*   **Message History**: The AI maintains a history of up to 8 messages for contextual understanding.

## 🎮 Games

Zyrix Bot features a collection of 12 standalone game engines, offering a diverse range of entertainment options to keep your community engaged and entertained.

### Available Games:

*   **Card Games**: Blackjack
*   **Casino Games**: Slots
*   **Strategy Games**: Chess, Connect Four, TicTacToe, Battleship
*   **Word Games**: Wordle, Country Guess, Type Racer
*   **Puzzle Games**: 2048, Number Slider, Lights Out
*   **Reaction Games**: Reaction Test, Memory Game
